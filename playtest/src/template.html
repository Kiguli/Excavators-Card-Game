    How to play

    The first player with 3 Gems of their own colour in their Warchest wins at once. The mine has 80 cards: 3 red and 3 blue Gems, 47 Rocks, 16 Treasures, 6 Cave-ins and 5 Junk. Each player has their own 17-card tool deck, including 2 Drills, and starts with 3 tools. These are the default rules; the setup screen has other options to playtest.

    1. Dig. Take the top card of the mine. If it's a Gem, either colour, show it, put it on the bottom of the mine, and dig again. Gems are won from the row, with a 9-Rock trade, or with a Drill.
    2. Main. Play tools and make Rock trades in any order: 3 Rocks draw a tool, 5 draw 2, 7 draw 3, and 9 Rocks find one of your Gems (then the mine is shuffled). A Drill digs the top card of the mine, and you keep it if it is your Gem. A Gold Nugget counts as 3 Rocks.
    3. Excavate. Lay the top 6 cards in a row.
    4. Roll. Roll 2 dice. Your rival may now play Claim Jump to take over this roll. Then choose one die. Your rival may change it with a Magnet, then you may answer. You dig the card at that position. A rival's Gem stays in the row.
    5. Return. Put the rest of the row on the bottom of the mine in any order. This is where you bury your rival's Gems.

    Cave-in: discard it and one of your tools. A Canary ignores it.
