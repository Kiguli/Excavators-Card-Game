    How to play

    The first player with 3 Gems of their own colour in their Warchest wins at once. The mine has 80 cards: 3 red and 3 blue Gems, 32 Rocks, 16 Treasures, 6 Cave-ins and 20 Junk. Each player has their own 15-card tool deck and starts with 1 tool.

    1. Dig. Take the top card of the mine. Your Gem goes to your Warchest and you dig again. A rival's Gem can't be kept by you: show it, put it on the bottom of the mine, and dig again.
    2. Main. Play tools and make Rock trades in any order: 3 Rocks draw a tool, 9 Rocks find one of your Gems (then the mine is shuffled). A Gold Nugget counts as 3 Rocks.
    3. Excavate. Lay the top 6 cards in a row. Your rival may play Claim Jump.
    4. Roll. Roll 2 dice and choose one. Your rival may change it with a Magnet, then you may answer. You dig the card at that position. A rival's Gem stays in the row.
    5. Return. Put the rest of the row on the bottom of the mine in any order. This is where you bury your rival's Gems.

    Cave-in: discard it and one of your tools. A Canary ignores it.
